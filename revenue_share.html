Revenue Distribution Overview

Below is our detailed revenue-sharing model including technical infrastructure costs, business share, and course provider's contribution. This transparent breakdown ensures clarity for all parties involved in the Guideray ecosystem.

Technical Infrastructure

30%

Covering all server, database, and technical maintenance costs to ensure platform reliability and performance.

Business Ownership

33%

Compensation for strategic leadership, business management, and overall platform ownership.

Course Provider

37%

Rewarding content creation, student support, and ongoing course maintenance efforts.
